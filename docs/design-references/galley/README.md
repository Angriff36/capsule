# Capsule visual reference: Galley

These screenshots are the visual north star for Capsule's product language. They are references for composition and character, not assets to reproduce or a mandate to make every page identical.

## What Capsule should carry forward

- A light, editorial workspace: warm white working surfaces framed by quiet botanical tones.
- Characterful serif display type for page titles and operational focal points; clean sans serif for controls and dense data.
- A slim, calm navigation rail that supports the work instead of dominating it.
- Fine rules, purposeful whitespace, small pills, and compact controls instead of a field of generic dashboard cards.
- Culinary specificity through language, rhythm, and restrained organic details.
- One clear focal point per page, with supporting information organized like a useful working document.
- Dense operational pages may be information-rich, but should still feel composed and legible.

## What to avoid

- Dark admin-template sidebars as the primary brand device.
- Four equally weighted KPI cards at the top of every page.
- Generic gradients, glass, glow, oversized radii, or decorative dashboard chrome.
- Fake metrics or imagery that suggests production data which does not exist.
- Copying Galley's logo, product-specific layouts, or proprietary content.
