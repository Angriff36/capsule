# Audience & Jobs to Be Done

<!-- Authored from capsule's verified audience (vision.md, docs/product/, the
     wants map) — same personas the complete-feature-spec was written for. -->

## Audience

### Josh — owner / operator (the caterer)

- _Context:_ Runs a catering company end to end, often from a phone between
  events. TPP (the legacy system) is still half-alive; Capsule must replace it
  without losing a booked event. Reviews money truth weekly (invoices, margins,
  Tim's KPIs).
- _Relationship:_ Owns the data staff and clients see; the only person who can
  make product decisions, so loop blockers must escalate to GitHub issues with
  the decision named.

### Kayden — field staff (BOH/FOH crew, drivers)

- _Context:_ Phone-only, on site, gloves on. Works from My Day, prep sheets,
  pack lists, event-day board. Cannot fill long forms or chase policy denials.
- _Relationship:_ Executes what Josh sells and plans; their clocked time and
  check-ins are the labor truth finance bills against.

### Sales staff — selling and planning events

- _Context:_ Turn inquiries into proposals, proposals into booked events.
  Live in the event spine (quote → planning → approval → sales lock →
  execution → closeout) and the CRM (leads, clients, communications).
- _Relationship:_ Hand finished plans to kitchen/logistics; hand accepted
  proposals to finance.

### Clients (prospects and booked customers)

- _Context:_ See only public/token surfaces: the quote form, share links,
  the client portal, signatures. Mobile browsers.
- _Relationship:_ Their acceptances and RSVPs drive the event spine forward.

### Tim — ownership/leadership dashboards

- _Context:_ Reads scorecards, L10, revenue trends, food-cost % — defined in
  the existing TPP report playbook, not re-invented.
- _Relationship:_ Consumes what the event spine and finance record; never
  enters data.

## Jobs to Be Done

### Josh

- _JTBD:_ Turn an accepted proposal into a fully-staffed, stocked, priced event
  without re-entering data — _so that_ nothing a client agreed to is lost
  between selling and serving (the #141 class of failure).
- _JTBD:_ See real money truth (billed vs collected vs dead, margin per event)
  — _so that_ weekly decisions come from Capsule, not TPP exports.
- _JTBD:_ Replace TPP completely, with imports that reconcile — _so that_ the
  parallel-run period ends and one system holds the truth.

### Kayden

- _JTBD:_ Know exactly what to prep, pack, and serve next from a phone —
  _so that_ the event runs without asking a manager.
- _JTBD:_ Clock in/out and record work without fighting the form — _so that_
  pay is right and no one re-keys timesheets.

### Sales staff

- _JTBD:_ Send a branded, priced proposal and get a signature — _so that_ the
  booking is locked the same day the client says yes.
- _JTBD:_ See every open lead and its next action — _so that_ no inquiry
  dies in a DM or inbox.

### Clients

- _JTBD:_ Price, accept, and pay for their event from a phone — _so that_
  booking doesn't require a phone call.
- _JTBD:_ See their event details, documents, and invoices in one portal —
  _so that_ they stop emailing to ask what's happening.

### Tim

- _JTBD:_ Open one dashboard and know if the month is on target — _so that_
  leadership meetings use live data instead of spreadsheets.
